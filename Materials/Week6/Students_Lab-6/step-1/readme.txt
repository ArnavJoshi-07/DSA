You are given a priority queue implemented using an ordered list having 
two pointers in its structure front and rear.
Modify so that there is only one pointer in the structure.
Make dequeue efficient.
Enqueue will require a loop.

The priority queue should have job - about filling pot holes in our city.
Each job has the depth of the pothole - indicated by a number from 1 to 5 
- higher the number, higher the depth
and traffic heaviness on the road - indicated by a number from 1 to 5
- higher the number, higher the severity.
The actual priority is obtained as a sum of these two.

Suitably modify the job to reflect the priority.
