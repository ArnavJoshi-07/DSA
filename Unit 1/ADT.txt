Abstract Data Type: 
> Can perform operations
> Can store various components
> Components are highly cohesive: Responsible for knowing and managing its own data.
> Does not need to know whether it will be stored in : 
    > Stack
    > Queue
    > Linked List
    > Priority Queue 
    > etc.

> High Cohesion: Each module and component has a focused responsibility and keeps 
                closely related responsibilities together
> Low coupling: Different modules/components have minimal 
dependency on each others internal implementation.
